# Nurul-Afser


Founder of EnamelPinStorage.com

Nurul Afser is a collector, researcher, and the founder of EnamelPinStorage.com, a resource dedicated to helping collectors store, organize, preserve, and display enamel pins and patches effectively. With nearly a decade of hands-on collecting experience, he specializes in evaluating storage solutions under real-world environmental conditions.

His collecting journey began in 2017 in Chittagong, Bangladesh, where high humidity, monsoon weather, and coastal air created unique preservation challenges. After experiencing issues such as mildew, warped display boards, and metal corrosion, he started systematically testing storage products and archival materials to identify solutions that genuinely protect collections over the long term.

After earning a Computing degree from BRAC University in 2019, Nurul applied a data-driven and analytical approach to product evaluation. His reviews focus on practical factors such as durability, humidity resistance, accessibility, material quality, and long-term preservation performance.

Later, relocating to Los Angeles, California provided an opportunity to evaluate storage products in a completely different climate. This experience allowed him to study how storage materials perform under both high-humidity and dry-environment conditions, giving collectors insights that are rarely available from single-region testing.

Today, Nurul maintains a collection of over 400 enamel pins and is also an active patch collector. His work covers storage systems, display methods, archival practices, and collector-focused organization strategies. A core principle behind his work is editorial independence—every product reviewed is personally purchased and tested without sponsorship influence.

Through detailed testing and long-term evaluation, Nurul's mission is to help collectors protect the items they value while avoiding costly storage and preservation mistakes.
